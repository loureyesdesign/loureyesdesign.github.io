LOU REYES / 2026 TORONTO, CANADA
LOU REYES / GRAPHIC DESIGNER

Hello,
I'm Lou.

I'm a graphic designer based in Toronto, with a particular interest in typography, imagery, and visual storytelling.

I've always enjoyed art, especially drawing and digital imagery. Graphic design wasn't originally my first choice, but my curiosity about Photoshop and image editing eventually led me toward it.

My brother encouraged me to try making posters in Canva. I enjoyed experimenting with different ideas, and what started as something simple became an interest I wanted to explore further.

What I enjoy most is the freedom to create something my own. There's a satisfaction in seeing an idea become something I've made myself.

What
I do.

My practice moves between different disciplines, combining an interest in visual storytelling with typography, imagery, and composition.

Editorial Design

Publications, layouts, and typography.

Visual Identity

Branding, identity systems, and art direction.

Packaging

Product presentation and packaging design.

Digital Experiences

Websites, interfaces, and digital design.

Photography

Architecture, nature, and visual observations.

Poster Design

Expressive compositions, typography, and imagery.

Beyond
the screen.

Outside of design, my interests include film, video games, music, fashion, photography, and architecture.

These interests influence the things I notice, from the lighting in a photograph to the typography of a magazine or the composition of a film poster.

I enjoy experimenting with different styles rather than limiting myself to one particular aesthetic. Professionally, I'm especially interested in poster design and fashion editorial work, where imagery and typography come together.
